Private accounting automation for White Investments Corporation. It converts Silverware daily sales reports into balanced journal entries for the company's QuickBooks Online account.

Privacy · Internal use terms · Connect · Disconnect

Privacy notice

Operator and contact: White Investments Corporation. Questions and requests: paulo.soupsup@gmail.com.

Purpose: This integration is for internal bookkeeping for Soup's Up Sticky Fingers (also reported as Soups Up) and ME Thyme. It is not offered to the public.

Data used: The app selects matching Silverware CSV report attachments in the authorized Gmail mailbox. It processes report dates, stores, check identifiers, sales, tax, tips and tender totals. From QuickBooks it reads company settings, accounts, locations and journal entries to validate and prevent duplicate postings. It creates daily journal entries after validation. It does not initiate payments, transfer money or process payroll.

Services and access: Google Apps Script runs the automation; Gmail supplies attachments and delivers operational alerts; Google Drive holds source files, generated journals and processing records. Intuit provides the authorized QuickBooks accounting API. Only the company's authorized administrator operates the app. Report contents are not sent to a generative AI service during scheduled processing, sold, or used for advertising. The integration has no analytics trackers.

Credentials and security: Intuit authorization uses OAuth. The app does not collect a QuickBooks password. Access and refresh tokens and the connected company identifier are encrypted with AES-256-GCM in the administrator's private Apps Script user properties. The encryption key and application credentials are restricted configuration, outside the code. The administrator uses Google two-step verification. The app communicates with Google and Intuit over HTTPS. Access to the project and Drive folders must remain restricted to authorized company personnel.

Retention: Source reports, journal CSVs and audit records remain in the company's Drive until the administrator removes them under the company's record-retention requirements. This version does not automatically delete accounting records. Disconnecting stops API access; it does not delete reports, audit records or journals already recorded in QuickBooks. Deleting processing records can undermine duplicate detection, so the administrator must stop the integration and review the effect first.

Location and requests: Google and Intuit operate the hosting services; this integration does not promise storage in a specific country. The administrator can request access, correction or removal through the contact above, subject to applicable accounting retention obligations. Changes to this notice require owner review before publication.

Internal use terms

This tool is intended only for authorized internal use by White Investments Corporation. It is not a subscription service or an app for third-party customers. The company retains ownership and control of its business records.

The administrator is responsible for authorizing the correct QuickBooks company, maintaining accurate account and location mappings, retaining required records and reviewing alerts. Changes to accounting rules require review and testing before use.

The tool validates reports and attempts journal creation one entry at a time. An error blocks subsequent automatic sends until reviewed. An entry already accepted by QuickBooks remains recorded if a later entry fails. An uncertain network outcome is held for reconciliation rather than automatically resent. Alerts depend on Gmail availability and delivery.

The tool does not replace professional accounting advice or the company's reconciliation process. Availability depends on Google, Intuit and Silverware. The administrator may pause imports or disconnect access at any time. Disconnecting does not reverse existing journal entries.

The tool itself has no subscription fee. Existing service subscriptions remain the company's responsibility; any future paid service or plan change requires separate approval. These internal terms do not replace the agreements the company has with Google, Intuit or Silverware.

Connect or reconnect

The authorized administrator starts the OAuth connection from the private Apps Script project, reviews the Intuit permission screen and selects White Investments Corporation. Connection alone does not enable journal posting: company validation, a preview and activation checks must be completed first. Contact the administrator above for assistance.

Disconnect

Pause imports in the private Apps Script project, then disconnect this application from the connected-app settings in QuickBooks. The administrator also removes the encrypted token from the script. Historical accounting records remain available. To reconnect, use the authorization procedure above.
